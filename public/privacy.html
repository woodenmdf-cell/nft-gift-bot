Maxfiylik siyosati — Shar Yutar

Shar Yutar — onlayn o'yin (Telegram Mini App, Android va iOS ilovasi). Bu sahifa qanday ma'lumot to'planishi va nima uchun ishlatilishini tushuntiradi.

Qanday ma'lumot to'playmiz

  • Telegram orqali kirsangiz: Telegram ID, ism, foydalanuvchi nomi (username). Profil rasmi faqat admin panelda ko'rsatish uchun Telegram'dan olinadi va saqlanmaydi.
  • O'yin ma'lumotlari: tokenlar, ichki balans, skinlar, rekord, rank (RP), sozlamalar (grafika, FPS).
  • Mehmon rejimi: hech qanday shaxsiy ma'lumot saqlanmaydi.
  • Reklama beruvchilar: reklama joylash uchun tasdiqlangan telefon raqami.
  • Reklama statistikasi: banner ko'rilgani va bosilgani (anonim sanoq uchun).
  • Ovozli chat (faqat Telegram versiyasida): ovoz faqat real vaqtda boshqa o'yinchilarga uzatiladi, serverda yozib olinmaydi va saqlanmaydi.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

O'yinni ishlatish, hisobingizni saqlash, reytinglar, firibgarlikka qarshi kurash va reklama statistikasi uchun. Ma'lumotlaringizni sotmaymiz va uchinchi shaxslarga bermaymiz (Telegram platformasining o'zidan tashqari).

Saqlash

Ma'lumotlar himoyalangan serverda saqlanadi. Akkauntni o'chirsangiz, ma'lumotlaringiz darhol o'chiriladi.

Akkauntni o'chirish

Ilovada: Hisob → Akkauntni o'chirish. Yoki Telegram'da @VIIZUAL_BOT ga /ochirish yozing. Batafsil: /delete-account.

Bolalar

Privacy Policy — Shar Yutar

What we collect: if you sign in with Telegram — your Telegram ID, first name and username; game progress (tokens, in-game balance, skins, best score, rank, graphics settings). Guests: nothing personal. Advertisers: a verified phone number. Banner views/clicks are counted anonymously. Voice chat (Telegram version only) is relayed live and never recorded or stored.

Why: to run the game, save your progress, show leaderboards, prevent cheating and count ad statistics. We do not sell or share your data with third parties (other than the Telegram platform itself).

Deletion: in the app go to Account → Delete account, or send /ochirish to @VIIZUAL_BOT. Data is deleted immediately. See /delete-account.

Children: the game is intended for users aged 13+. The store app contains no purchases and no prizes.
